Frequently Asked Questions

How does GhostGlyph hide messages in emojis?
Every character of your secret message is converted into a 16-bit binary sequence. Each bit is mapped to a non-rendering Unicode zero-width character (Zero-Width Joiner for 1, Zero-Width Non-Joiner for 0) and attached behind your chosen carrier emojis. To any human eye or text renderer, only the carrier is visible.
Can third parties or algorithms detect the hidden message?
Standard OCR scanners, casual observers, and social media feeds will only display the normal emoji. Only someone inspecting the raw UTF-8 byte stream or pasting the text into a steganography extractor like GhostGlyph will uncover the hidden payload.
Does GhostGlyph store or transmit my messages?
Never. GhostGlyph operates entirely inside your local web browser. No analytics track your payloads, and no server API calls are ever made when encoding or decoding.
